A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Practical by experience.

I’m Cyrus Alcala, an independent digital maker in Manila. I design, build and connect things for the internet.

It starts with understanding the work.

My background is in customer operations and technical writing. Insurance claims, support queues, knowledge articles and process documentation taught me to look closely at what people are trying to do—and where the information stops helping.

That perspective stays with me when I build a website or an AI tool. A clear interface matters. So does the system behind it, the explanation that makes it usable, and what happens when something goes wrong.

One practice, several forms.

Websites are my commercial focus. Video, social content and automation are other ways I help a business communicate and work. My independent products let me explore those ideas in public.

I recently completed the Google AI Professional Certificate through Coursera. I keep learning through building, testing and documenting the results.
