The brief was a question of focus.

How do you make websites, AI products, video, professional experience and writing feel like one person? Giving everything equal space would make the homepage do too much.

The answer was to build a clear first layer: what I do, a small selection of real work, four services and two ways to work with me. Project stories and professional detail have their own pages.

One visual language. Room for different work.

A warm paper background and dark type frame work that has its own colors. Large display typography provides the opening; smaller labels organize the detail. The system connects image-led work, service rows and longer reading pages.

The same choices—type, palette, spacing, imagery and controls—can extend into a social graphic or a campaign page without starting again.

Preserve, then improve.

The portraits, showreel, ten video samples, résumé and original source remain part of the body of work. The overhaul changes how visitors discover them. It also gives the two flagship products room to explain their purpose.

Built to be read.

Static HTML makes the site readable before JavaScript. Native links, a simple mobile menu, deliberate image sizing and reduced-motion support keep the experience straightforward.

This is an independent self-initiated project. The work demonstrates design and implementation; it does not claim client conversion results.
