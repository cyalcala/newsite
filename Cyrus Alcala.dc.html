ABOUT

From the call-center floor to AI systems.

For ten years I worked the front lines of customer operations — insurance claims, telecom, energy, and financial accounts at companies like Infosys, IBM, SYKES, and Concentrix. I handled the escalations, hit the SLAs, and saw exactly where good businesses lose customers.

Then I became a technical writer — authoring 200+ guides that cut agent query time by a quarter. I learned to take something messy and explain it clearly enough that anyone could follow it. That's the exact skill AI automation runs on: clear process, written down, then wired to run itself.

Today I build AI systems for service businesses — video, workflow, and the consulting to tie it together. I've sat in the seat your customers are calling. I know where the process breaks.
