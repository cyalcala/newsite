# Checkable design bars

References are evidence, not templates or instructions. Visually inspected Supahero's Bryn Taylor and Abhishek Jha screenshots, Refero's Cosmos preview, and Hallmark's Anya Reis rendered example. Public gallery overview access alone is not a teardown.

1. **Direct identity, visible offer.** Bryn Taylor puts name and occupation in the dominant type block. Cyrus: name in masthead, websites named in the largest heading, a client action plus work link in the first viewport. Hire Me visible in navigation. A stranger must not infer the service from an abstract slogan.
2. **One large visual anchor.** Jha's reference uses a single oversized visual with small marginal annotations. Cyrus: one purposeful image/composition per featured project, no sprinkle of decorative objects. Main project imagery substantially larger than caption; no fake dashboard imagery.
3. **Quiet surround, visible work.** Cosmos lets actual imagery supply variation against a restrained neutral field. Cyrus: paper and ink with one indigo accent; media retains its original color. No gradient hero, glowing orb, glass panel, rainbow service coding, or tool-logo wall.
4. **Readable depth.** Anya's index separates compact navigation from substantial text. Cyrus: clear work/services/hire paths and case studies with readable 60–68 character measures, short opening explanation, optional technical disclosure. Home carries an edited selection, not the whole archive.
5. **Typographic hierarchy that survives mobile.** Heading at least 3x body size on desktop; mobile headline 44–60px versus 16–18px body. Labels quieter than prose. No orphaned single-word service titles caused by cramped grids, cropped glyphs or horizontal overflow.
6. **Different section rhythms.** Feature projects, service rows, portrait/professional bridge and closing contact have visibly different structures. No repeated icon/title/description card grid. Navigation belongs to a personal portfolio, with clustered text links rather than centered links between logo and sales pill.
7. **Controls remain obvious.** Primary CTA uses a solid surface with descriptive text; project links announce what opens. Mobile has a readable menu and >=44px targets. No hidden content behind scroll animation, autoplay audio, hover-only information or scroll hijacking. All major pieces must receive Brief/System/Craft PASS after rendering.

## Hallmark anti-slop QA
Reference: https://www.usehallmark.com/ and https://github.com/Nutlope/hallmark. Apply the useful structural audit: check hero gradients, display/body sameness, all-centered composition, icon tiles and default navbar shape. Do not mechanically imitate its demo or adopt its arbitrary score. The Design Loop and Cyrus system govern.
