ABOUT

The skills that kept a call center running now build the automation that replaces the busywork.

I spent ten years in customer operations at Infosys, IBM, SYKES, and Concentrix. Insurance claims, telecom escalations, financial account audits. I learned exactly where service businesses break down, and what it costs them when they do.

Then I became a technical writer and authored 200+ process guides that cut agent query time by 25%. That's the exact skill AI automation runs on: take something messy and explain it clearly enough that anyone can follow it — human or machine.

Now I build AI systems for service businesses — video, workflow automation, and the strategy to connect them. I bring the operator's perspective because I've sat in the seat your customers are calling. I know exactly where the process breaks.
